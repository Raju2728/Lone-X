## Held-out evaluation

Three unseen prompts were evaluated with both the unchanged base and adapter,
using greedy generation capped at 96 new tokens. All six generations were
nonempty. The routine and file-request responses were unchanged and garbled;
the explanation changed but remained garbled. Some outputs were repetitive.
Nonempty output is not a quality pass, and no fluency score is claimed.

Full output: `test-artifacts/training-qwen-smoke-evaluation.json`.
