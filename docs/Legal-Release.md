# Legal policy release notes

The website and desktop app read the same [policy source](../desktop/legal-documents.json). The website exposes the Terms & Conditions at `/terms` and Privacy Policy at `/privacy`. The desktop first-run screen and **Settings → Legal & Policies** also show the EULA.

The founder supplied `lonex.chat@gmail.com` as the privacy and grievance contact. Before publication, confirm that the inbox is monitored and confirm the contracting operator's legal name and location. Confirm the intended repository license and contributor permissions separately. The EULA applies to Lone X's distributed contributions and preserves applicable third-party and prior license grants. Confirm Vercel logging and retention settings, actual support handling, and the approved effective date. Have Indian counsel review the policies, including mandatory consumer remedies, privacy duties, and any obligations in other markets served. Check the notices for the exact desktop package and model versions that will ship.

Change the version of each revised document. For changes that require renewed agreement, also increment `consentRevision`. Keep a release archive of the text corresponding to each accepted version. Minor editorial changes can retain the same consent revision. The receipt records the revision, document versions, and UTC time in protected local storage. It is per Windows app profile. A prior profile that has never accepted must accept once after updating.

The privacy acknowledgment confirms notice. Obtain any legally required consent for optional processing separately. Before enabling a new remote provider, telemetry, marketing, or hosted account processing, update the policy and implement the appropriate user controls.
